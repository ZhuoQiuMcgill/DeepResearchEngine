# Next School Years Begin Between January and September 2027

The next school year starts anywhere from late January 2027 to early September 2027, depending on the country and, in most cases, on the local body that sets the calendar. South Africa's public schools open first, on **13 January 2027**, under one national calendar ([FundiConnect](https://fundiconnect.co.za/south-african-school-calendar-2027)). Australian states start in late January or early February 2027 ([Simple Calendar](https://www.simplecalendar.com.au/blog/school-holidays-2027-parents-guide)). In the United States, England, Wales and Germany, the next start falls in **August or early September 2027**, though the exact day varies by district, council or state. India has no confirmed national date: the CBSE session is said to begin on **1 April 2027**, but that figure is a projection from a countdown site, and state boards reopen anywhere from late May to early July ([TimerKit](https://timerkit.app/countdown/back-to-school/india/cbse)). For any one school, the only reliable answer is the date published by that school's governing body. Universities run on their own calendars and usually start later.

## What the question's terms mean in practice

The "first day" in this answer means the day students return to class under the published calendar. It is distinct from the teachers' report day: in South Africa, **educators report on 11 January 2027**, two days before learners start on 13 January ([FundiConnect](https://fundiconnect.co.za/south-african-school-calendar-2027)). The "next school year" means the first year whose start falls after 20 November 2026. In England, the 2026-27 year is already under way, so the next one is 2027-28; in South Africa and Australia, the next start is the 2027 Term 1 start. The sources do not give a legal definition of "school year." The working definition used across the sources is the calendar a school's governing body publishes, which can differ from the year as a national statistic would count it.

## Australia and South Africa restart in January and February

South Africa is the clearest case: the Minister of Basic Education published the 2027 calendar as **Government Notice 5902 on 25 February 2025**, and all nine provinces follow it ([gov.za](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/202502/52178gon5902.pdf); [Find SA Status](https://findsastatus.co.za/education/school-calendar-2027/)). Public schools close on 8 December 2027, though one site gives 10 December, so the gazette is the document to check ([Back to School SA](https://www.backtoschool.co.za/school-calendar/2027)). Independent schools set their own dates ([Kayawise](https://days.kayawise.co.za/school-terms-2027/)).

Australia has no national start date. Queensland, South Australia and the Northern Territory start on 27 January 2027, Victoria on 28 January, Western Australia on 1 February, the ACT on 2 February, New South Wales on 3 February and Tasmania on 4 February ([Holidays and Dates](https://holidaysanddates.com.au/back-to-school/); [Teach Buy Sell](https://teachbuysell.com.au/teacher-guides/school-term-dates-2027)). The sources conflict on several of these. One site lists NSW Term 1 as 27 January ([Apollo Templates](https://www.apollostemplates.com/calendars/australian-school-terms/)) and another as 29 January ([Australian School Holidays](https://australianschoolholidays.online/school-term-dates-2027/)), while most give 3 February. One lists the Western Division of NSW starting on 10 February ([Cuemath](https://www.cuemath.com/blog/school-holidays-2027-australia/)). Because none of these figures comes from a state education department page, the differences of a few days remain unresolved.

## Northern and US systems open in August and September 2027

The United States has no federal school start date, and districts set their own calendars. Aggregator tables put most 2027-28 first days between early August and early September, with Hawaii on 2 August, Florida on 10 August, Delaware on 30 August and Minnesota on 7 September ([schoolholidays.com](https://schoolholidays.com/guides/when-does-school-start-in-america/)). District-approved dates confirm the spread: **Gaffney, South Carolina, on 5 August 2027** ([The Gaffney Ledger](https://www.gaffneyledger.com/articles/school-trustees-adopt-2027-28-calendar-start-date-august-5/)), and **Anne Arundel County, Maryland, on 30 August 2027** ([AACPS](https://www.aacps.org/article/2874544/)). Frisco ISD in Texas and Allen ISD also set August starts through board-approved calendars ([Community Impact](https://communityimpact.com/frisco/education/frisco-isd-sets-academic-calendar-for-2027-28-school-year/); [Community Impact](https://communityimpact.com/allen/education/allen-isd-board-approves-2027-28-academic-calendar/)). A single US figure would mislead, because the gap between early and late districts is more than three weeks.

In England, Wales and Northern Ireland, the 2026-27 year is already running, so the next start is in September 2027. Several English councils list the autumn term starting **Wednesday 1 September 2027**, for example [Barnsley](https://www.barnsley.gov.uk/services/schools-and-education/school-terms-and-holidays/). Welsh councils mostly match, though Carmarthenshire lists 2 September ([Carmarthenshire](https://www.carmarthenshire.gov.wales/council-services/education-schools/school-term-dates/)). Scotland starts earlier: Glasgow lists pupils returning on **18 August 2027** ([Glasgow City Council](https://www.glasgow.gov.uk/article/4276/School-Term-Dates)), but council dates range from about 11 to 24 August across Scotland ([calendar-uk](https://www.calendar-uk.co.uk/school-calendars/scotland/2027-2028/)). No source found gives an official Northern Ireland first day for 2027-28, and the Welsh Government's approved-dates page was not readable enough to confirm a single date.

Germany sets its holidays state by state, so the return to school in autumn 2027 also varies. The summer holiday ends on **6 August 2027** in Hessen, Rheinland-Pfalz and Saarland, and on **13 September 2027** in Bayern ([Schulferienplaner](https://schulferienplaner.de/ferien/sommerferien-2027)). The first school day in each state most likely follows the holiday's end, but no source states this directly. German universities are different: the winter semester formally runs from 1 October, with lectures usually starting in mid-October ([Germany for Students](https://germanyforstudents.com/en/blog/semester-dates-germany)).

## India's date is projected and split across boards

India is the weakest case. The CBSE session is said to run from 1 April to 31 March, with schools told not to start before 1 April ([Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/cbse-academic-session-start-from-april-1-board-asks-schools-follow)). The same TimerKit page, however, says the 2027-28 calendar has not been published and that the 1 April date is projected, so the source conflicts with itself ([TimerKit](https://timerkit.app/countdown/back-to-school/india/cbse)). A holiday-list aggregator puts most Indian schools' reopening in June, with South Indian schools possibly in early June ([Padhaao](https://padhaao.in/blog/school-holiday-list-2027-28-month-wise-calendar-states)). Individual private schools diverge further: Shrewsbury India lists its 2027-28 year starting **23 August 2027** ([Shrewsbury India](https://shrewsburyindia.in/academics/academic-dates/)). No official CBSE, ministry or state-board calendar for 2027-28 was available to confirm any of these dates.

## Confirming a specific school's start date

The body that governs a school publishes the authoritative date. In England, the gov.uk term-dates service points families to council websites, and academies publish their own dates ([TermDatesAPI](https://termdatesapi.co.uk/); [Time and Date](https://time-and-date.uk.com/uk-school-holiday-dates)). Academies, free schools and some other types can set their own term dates, so a school's date may differ from its council's ([UK Parliament Commons Library](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/SN07148/)). In the United States, the district calendar is the source, and boards can revise approved calendars: Poway Unified moved its 2026-27 first day from 12 August to 13 August ([Poway USD](https://www.powayusd.com/apps/news/article/2173374?categoryId=19992)). In Australia, state education departments publish the dates, and Catholic and independent schools may differ ([Minimode](https://minimode.com.au/guides/school-term-dates-2026)).

Publication lead times are generally long. English councils usually publish two or three years ahead ([Schools Insight](https://schoolsinsight.co.uk/blog/school-term-dates-explained)), and Bay Village City Schools in Ohio approved its 2028-29 calendar in October 2026 ([Bay Village City Schools](https://www.bayk12.org/o/bms/article/3203943/)). Universities set their own dates. Rice's Fall 2026 classes begin 24 August 2026 ([Rice Registrar](https://registrar.rice.edu/calendars/fall-semester-2026)), and Columbia's Fall 2027 classes begin 7 September 2027 ([Columbia Registrar](https://registrar.columbia.edu/content/academic-calendar)). A family asking about a school should not use a university calendar, even if a sibling is at university.

## Conclusion

The honest answer to "when does the school year start?" is that it depends on the country and, within it, on the local body. For most families reading this in late 2026, the next start is in 2027: January or February in South Africa and Australia, and August or September in the United States, England, Wales, Scotland and Germany. The clearest and most authoritative date is South Africa's gazetted calendar. Australia's and the United States' figures are mostly consistent but still carry a few-day conflict, and India's 1 April date is a projection rather than an official calendar.

The practical step is to check the governing body's own published calendar: the district, council, academy trust, state department or school itself. Those calendars are usually published well ahead of time, but boards and councils do revise them, so the date should be checked again close to the start.
