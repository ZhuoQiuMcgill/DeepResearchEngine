# Most Major School Systems Open in August or September 2027

The next start for most large school systems is in late August or early-to-mid September 2027, but the answer depends on hemisphere and on who sets the calendar. In the Northern Hemisphere, the 2026–27 year has already begun almost everywhere, so the next start is 2027–28, typically between **August 9 and September 14, 2027** across the systems researched. The Southern Hemisphere and parts of Africa have a different cycle: the next start is in **January or early February 2027**, and that is the earliest next start of any major system. No single worldwide date exists. Start dates are set by national governments in some countries (France, Poland, and Saudi Arabia's Cabinet), by states or provinces in others (Australia, Germany, India, China), and by districts, councils or schools in the US, UK and Ireland. Several key dates are still unpublished, and some sources conflict. The most reliable pattern is that the 2027 Northern Hemisphere start falls in the last third of August or the first half of September, while the Southern Hemisphere opens in the last weeks of January or early February.

## Northern Hemisphere systems open late August to mid-September

The largest US districts with adopted 2027–28 calendars start early-to-late August. **Hillsborough County, Florida, opens on August 10, 2027** ([School Board Approves 2027-2028 Student Academic Calendar](https://www.hillsboroughschools.org/article/3031798/)), **Frisco ISD in Texas opens on August 11, 2027** ([Frisco ISD Board Approves 2027-28 Instructional Calendar](https://www.friscoisd.org/o/fisd/article/3258749)), and **Chicago Public Schools starts on August 23, 2027** ([Chalkbeat](https://www.chalkbeat.org/chicago/2026/02/26/cps-school-board-adopts-new-school-calendars/)). Los Angeles Unified is listed for **August 12, 2027** by several aggregators ([Los Angeles Unified Calendar (schoolstartdates)](https://schoolstartdates.com/california/los-angeles-unified/)), though other sites give August 15–17, which appear unreliable. There is no federal start date: the US guide notes that [each state and local district decides its own calendar](https://clevelandesdc.com/when-does-a-school-year-start/). A Pew sample cited by the press found that only **18% of districts** start after Labor Day ([The Center Square](https://www.thecentersquare.com/issues/education/article_39cc800b-d846-5cb3-86a2-dc9e64d93913.html)). New York City is the main exception: its 2026–27 year began on [September 10, 2026](https://portal.311.nyc.gov/article/?kanumber=KA-02522&washelpful=True), and its 2027–28 calendar has not been released, with one projection giving [September 9, 2027](https://www.nyschoolcalendar.com/guides/how-we-projected-nyc-2027-2028-school-calendar/).

Outside the US, the pattern is similar. England's councils most commonly start on **Wednesday, September 1, 2027**, as listed by [Stockport Council](https://www.stockport.gov.uk/school-term-dates/2027-to-2028-term-dates) and [Royal Greenwich](https://www.royalgreenwich.gov.uk/schools-and-education/school-term-dates/term-dates-2027-2028). Scottish councils start about two weeks earlier, with [Glasgow City Council](https://www.glasgow.gov.uk/article/4276/School-Term-Dates) listing **August 18, 2027**. In Ireland, the Department of Education's [Circular 0018/2026](https://assets.gov.ie/static/documents/2602a673/2026.01.30_FINAL_Circular_SSY_2026.29_30_Jan_2026_English.pdf) standardises the 2027/28 breaks, but each school sets its own first day, so no national opening date exists. In Ontario, the [Ministry's 2027–28 calendar](https://www.ontario.ca/files/2024-08/edu-school-year-calendar-2027-28-en-2024-08-23.pdf) allows 197 possible school days from September 1, 2027, and [TimerKit projects a return window of 31 August to 8 September 2027](https://timerkit.app/countdown/back-to-school/canada/ontario), though no board calendar has been published. Only Ontario was researched among Canadian provinces.

## Europe's start dates are set nationally in a few countries and regionally elsewhere

France and Poland have the clearest single national dates. France's 2027/28 calendar was [set by order on 21 July 2026](https://www.faire-le-pont.fr/vacances-scolaires/rentree-2027/), and students return on **Thursday, September 2, 2027** ([L'Etudiant](https://www.letudiant.fr/lifestyle/vacances-scolaires-2027-2028-le-calendrier-devoile-avec-toutes-les-dates.html)). Poland starts classes on the first working day of September under a regulation, which gives [September 1, 2027](https://www.kalbi.pl/kalendarz-szkolny-2027-2028). Russia's Ministry of Enlightenment set the [2026/27 start at September 1, 2026](https://vladnews.ru/2026-07-09/271016/minprosveshcheniya_utverdilo), and no source states the 2027/28 date explicitly.

Germany, Spain and the Netherlands show wide spreads because their regions set dates. In Germany, the first school day for 2027/28 ranges from **August 9 in Hesse, Rhineland-Palatinate and Saarland** to **September 14 in Bavaria**, according to [ferienradar.com](https://www.ferienradar.com/schuljahr/beginn-2027-2028/); those figures come from an aggregator, not from Land ministries. In the Netherlands, the legal school year runs from August 1 to July 31, but the first day for 2027/28 is **August 23 (North), August 30 (Central) or September 6 (South)** ([datum.nl](https://www.datum.nl/vakanties/schooljaar)). In Spain, [Catalonia's official calendar](https://web.gencat.cat/en/ciutadania/educacio-formacio/calendari-escolar-catalunya) gives **September 8, 2027**, and most other communities had not published 2027/28 calendars when checked. Belgium's Flemish schools open on [September 1, 2027](https://www.delaatsteminuut.be/pagina/schoolvakanties), and Wallonia-Brussels on [August 30, 2027](https://www.coddygames.com/en/family-friends/blog/lifestyle/2027-2028-school-calendar-wallonia-brussels). Italy's regions set their own dates, and the 2027/28 calendars are not yet published; for 2026/27 the start ranged from 7 to 17 September 2026 ([newsistruzione.it](https://www.newsistruzione.it/2026/07/08/calendario-scolastico-2026-2027-inizio-lezioni)).

## Southern Hemisphere and African systems start in January and February

Because the Southern Hemisphere's 2026 year ended in December, its next start is the first in the world after today. In Australia, the states differ by up to two weeks. NSW's [government page](https://www.nsw.gov.au/about-nsw/school-holidays) gives Term 1 2027 as starting **Wednesday, February 3** in the Eastern division and **Monday, February 10** in the Western division. Victoria's [government page](https://www.vic.gov.au/school-term-dates-and-holidays-victoria) gives **January 27** for Term 1, with government school students starting January 28. Queensland starts on **January 27, 2027** according to [Kisacademics](https://kisacademics.com/blog/qld-school-holidays-2027-term-dates-public-holidays/). New Zealand's Term 1 falls between **January 28 and February 3, 2027**, with each school choosing its day ([Calculate.co.nz](https://www.calculate.co.nz/reference/nz-school-terms.php)).

South Africa has one national calendar: the gazetted 2027 public school year begins **January 13** and ends **December 8**, applying to all nine provinces ([Teneo](https://www.teneoschool.co.za/blog/school-calendar-2027-south-africa-term-dates-holidays-and-long-weekends)). Kenya's Term One opens on **January 4, 2027** under a Ministry circular ([Arena Kenya](https://arena.co.ke/education-ministry-releases-2027-school-calendar-for-schools-and-colleges/)). Brazil's year typically begins in the first week of February and ends in early December ([Wikipedia, Academic term](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_term)), but no official 2027 date was retrieved.

## Asia and the Middle East follow mixed patterns

Start dates in Asia and the Middle East vary more by country and region. China's public schools usually start on **September 1**, and the next Beijing start is listed as [September 1, 2027](https://globalschoolcalendar.com/china/), though provincial bureaus set their own calendars. The Philippines' 2026–27 year opened on **June 8, 2026** under DepEd Order No. 009, with the [closing date set for April 8, 2027](https://www.philstar.com/headlines/2026/04/18/2521743/angara-classes-start-june-8); no 2027–28 opening has been published. Indonesia's year typically starts in mid-July, according to [Edarabia](https://www.edarabia.com/school-holidays-indonesia/), and a Jakarta international school projects [July 19, 2027](https://www.acsjakarta.sch.id/school-hours-and-academic-year-calendar).

India depends on the board and state. CBSE's session runs from April 1 to March 31, and a countdown site gives [April 1, 2027](https://timerkit.app/countdown/back-to-school/india/cbse) for its return. Most southern states use a June start, per [Padhaao](https://padhaao.in/blog/school-holiday-list-2027-28-month-wise-calendar-states). Saudi Arabia's 2026–27 year began on **August 23, 2026** ([Arabian Business](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/business/education/saudi-school-calendar-2026-27)), and the 2027–28 year is listed to start on **August 22, 2027**, with schools allowed to shift the start by up to two weeks ([Education Saudi](https://www.education-saudi.com/saudi-school-calendar-2026-2027/)). Israel's next year starts on **September 1, 2027** ([AX Calendar](https://axcalendar.info/eng/vacations/)), an unofficial aggregator. Turkey's 2026–27 year began on **September 14, 2026** ([RestProperty](https://restproperty.com/news-en/gizn-v-turcii/turkiye-official-school-calendar-2026-2027/)), and Egypt's public schools began on **September 12, 2026**, with international schools on September 6 ([Cairo Portal](https://cairo.gov.eg/en/news/2026/septemper/3/8b48023bf4f34936bbf325c2b55360f2)). No 2027–28 start was found for Turkey, Egypt or the UAE.

## Where the evidence is thin or conflicting

Several major systems have no usable next-start evidence. Japan, South Korea, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nigeria, Mexico, Argentina, Colombia and Chile were not covered by any source retrieved, and only Ontario was researched among Canadian provinces. Several notable dates conflict across sources. Victoria's Term 1 end date is [March 25 on the official page](https://vicparentscouncil.vic.edu.au/resources/school-term-dates) but [April 1 on Australian Public Holidays](https://australianpublicholidays.com/victoria-school-holidays-2027-calendar/). South Africa's final day is [December 8 in most sources](https://clubengage.co.za/2027-school-calendars/) but [December 10 in HolidayHub](https://holidayhub.co.za/school-terms/2027/). For Ontario, [TermBreaks lists a January 4, 2027 start](https://termbreaks.com/canada/ontario/2027), which conflicts with the Ministry's September framework and should not be relied on. Germany's Rheinland-Pfalz date is labelled provisional by one aggregator, and the German figures generally come from non-official sites.

## Conclusion

For a global answer, the most useful distinction is between the next start date and the next full-year start. Southern Hemisphere and African systems, including South Africa on January 13, 2027 and Kenya on January 4, 2027, open first. Most Northern Hemisphere systems open between late August and mid-September 2027. US districts with adopted calendars cluster around August 10–23, while New York City remains unresolved and is likely to start after Labor Day.

Readers planning around a specific system should check the authority that sets its calendar, because the spread within countries can be as large as the spread across them. The Germany, Italy, Australia, India and China figures vary by state or province, and the US figures vary by district. Where the evidence is aggregator-only, as with many German, Spanish and Indian dates, the dates should be treated as provisional until official publications appear.
