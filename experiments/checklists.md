# Coverage checklists for the comparison

Written before the comparison runs, from the question alone (Lab 5 review,
change 6). An item counts as covered only if the report states it **and** cites
a source that supports it. Judges score reports blind: file names are hashed,
and anything naming the arm or the model is removed first (`docs/design.md`
§12). Score each item 1 (stated and supported), ½ (stated, unsupported or
partly supported) or 0. Also record a wrong statement on an item: it counts
against the report even when cited.

The lists name what a full answer contains, not the answer itself. A report may
cover more; extra material is judged under quality, not coverage.

## ebd: `What is EBD (Environment Based Design)?`

1. Definition: EBD as a design methodology, with its originator and where and when it was introduced.
2. Its core idea: the environment of a product (natural, built, human) is the first thing to analyse, and design problems come from the environment.
3. The main activities and how they repeat: environment analysis, conflict identification, solution generation.
4. How EBD identifies conflicts, and what counts as one.
5. Its modelling tools: the Recursive Object Model (ROM) and how questions are asked from it.
6. Its theoretical basis (the axiomatic theory of design modelling) or the design questions it rests on.
7. Its life-cycle view of the environment (environment-based life-cycle decomposition).
8. Where it has been applied, with at least one example.
9. How it differs from other design methods, or its limitations.
10. The acronym: other meanings of "EBD" are kept apart from this one.

## headset: `compare meta quest 3 and apple vision pro`

1. Which versions are compared, as of the run date (later revisions or successors named if they exist).
2. Price and what it includes, in at least one market.
3. Availability: release dates and where each is sold.
4. Displays: resolution, panel type, field of view.
5. Processor and performance.
6. Passthrough and mixed-reality quality.
7. Input: controllers, hand and eye tracking.
8. Weight, comfort and battery life.
9. Software: operating system, app ecosystem, games against productivity and media.
10. Who each suits, or an overall verdict that follows from the points above.

## flight: `How do I flight from GuangZhou to Montreal?`

1. Whether nonstop flights operate, as of the run date.
2. The main one-stop routes: connecting hubs and the airlines serving them.
3. Typical total travel time.
4. Typical fares and where to book.
5. Entry documents for Canada for the main kinds of traveller (visa, eTA, visa-exempt), with how to apply.
6. Transit rules at the main connecting hubs (transit visas).
7. Departure from Guangzhou: airport, check-in and exit formalities.
8. Arrival in Montreal: airport, customs and immigration.
9. Getting from the Montreal airport into the city.
10. The traveller's unstated details (nationality, dates) are named as assumptions where they change the answer.

## sleep: `What food can help me sleep well?`

1. Specific foods or drinks with evidence of better sleep.
2. How they are thought to work (melatonin, tryptophan, magnesium and similar).
3. The strength of the evidence for each: trials against observational studies, sample sizes.
4. Amounts and timing, where studies give them.
5. Foods and drinks that harm sleep (caffeine, alcohol, heavy or late meals).
6. Overall dietary patterns, beyond single foods.
7. Safety notes: allergies, interactions, people for whom advice differs.
8. Limits: food is not a treatment for sleep disorders, and when to see a clinician.

## Process criterion for EBD runs (Lab 5 review, change 5)

Goal growth passes when every main part of the topic that the findings name is
covered by a goal and explained in the report. A part counts as named when a
finding line introduces it as a part, step or component of an object of the
question. A part that is named but has no goal fails the test even when the
report mentions it in passing. Check this in the run viewer: the findings of
each round against the goal table and the report.
